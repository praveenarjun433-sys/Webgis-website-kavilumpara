Introduction

About Kavilumpara Grama Panchayat

Kavilumpara Grama Panchayat is located in Kozhikode district in the northern part of Kerala, India. It is a rural area characterised by green landscapes, agricultural activities, settlements and hilly terrain associated with the Western Ghats region.

Agriculture forms an important part of the local economy. Plantation crops, rural settlements and transport routes contribute to the geographical and socioeconomic character of the area.

The region's connection to the Kuttiady Ghat route provides access towards the Wayanad highlands. Its terrain, rainfall and drainage patterns are important considerations for transportation, environmental conservation and disaster management.

This WebGIS website provides a digital platform to explore local geography and understand the relationship between natural resources, human activities and spatial development.

Geographical Profile

Geography and Natural Environment

Explore the major geographical characteristics of Kavilumpara and its surrounding region.

📍

Geographical Location

Kavilumpara is situated in Kozhikode district, Kerala. It is associated with Kunnummal Block Panchayat and the Vatakara taluk area.

⛰️

Relief and Topography

The wider region includes undulating and hilly terrain towards the Western Ghats. Relief influences settlement patterns, transport and surface drainage.

🌳

Forest and Biodiversity

Green cover and forested landscapes form an important part of the surrounding environment. Conservation helps protect biodiversity and natural resources.

🌧️

Climate and Rainfall

The region experiences a tropical monsoon-influenced climate. Seasonal rainfall affects agriculture, streams, slope stability and drainage.

🌱

Agriculture and Land Use

Agriculture is an important rural activity. Rubber, coconut and other crops contribute to the agricultural landscape of the region.

🛣️

Transport and Connectivity

The Kuttiady Ghat route connects this part of Kozhikode district with the Wayanad highlands. Hilly roads require attention to slope conditions and safe accessibility.
